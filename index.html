Five years across the mobile stack

I started in native Android at BJIT, building system apps, a translation app and the software for six of Pocketalk's translation devices. In 2024 I moved to Flutter at mPower, where I led 14 mobile developers shipping apps for farmers and rural communities across Bangladesh.

Today I'm a technical lead at the Bangladesh Army IT Directorate, responsible for 20 developers and for communication, tracking and healthcare systems where security comes first. I still maintain native Android and iOS codebases, which is why I'm comfortable going below the Flutter layer when a plugin doesn't exist.
